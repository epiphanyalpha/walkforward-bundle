How the bundle is built: for each in-sample window the candidate rules are ranked by the chosen metric, the best are filtered until pairwise correlation stays under the cap, and the survivors are equal-weighted over the following period. The out-of-sample legs are stitched together. Then it repeats at every point of the grid. No data leaves your browser: the computation runs in this tab. The method is specification curve analysis applied to back-tests.
